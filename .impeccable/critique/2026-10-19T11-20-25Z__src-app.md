### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Strong product-specific identity on the landing page and analysis results. The diagnostic-report metaphor is fully realized. Where specificity erodes: the authenticated app shell becomes generic single-column utility.

**Deterministic scan**: 6 findings. 3x side-tab (border-l-4 in AnalysisView, documented in DESIGN.md as intentional), 3x scrollbar false positives (raw CSS hex values and radius in ::-webkit-scrollbar rules). src/components scanned clean.

### Overall Impression

Fixes from the last round landed well. Error visibility, input validation, shared navigation, and loading feedback are measurably better. Score moved from 22 to 25. The biggest remaining opportunity: the product does not help the user act on its analysis.

### What's Working

1. The diagnostic-report metaphor is category-defining. The landing page hero IS the product output.
2. The copy is honest and precise. Error messages are specific and actionable. No filler.
3. The constraint discipline is real. Severity monopoly, navy-is-ink, one-document shadow — all followed.
