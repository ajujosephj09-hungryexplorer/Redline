# Landing Page

**Mode:** Persuade
**Audience:** Freelancers about to accept an inbound contract they cannot negotiate
**Job:** Understand what Redline does and try it on their document
**Action:** Upload a contract
**Constraints:** No invented prices, customers, or quotes. No legal advice. No enforceability claims. No scanned/image documents. Only the contract types PRD.md serves (service agreements, NDAs, IP assignments, payment schedules, kill-fee provisions).

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The contract receives a diagnostic workup — systematic, authoritative, every finding measured against a standard and cited to its source. The category-default SaaS hero with a screenshot and feature grid is refused; the report itself is the hero, the demonstration is the product.

**OWN-WORLD:** Clinical white ground. Deep navy (#1e293b) for structure and authority. Red (#dc2626) for critical findings, amber (#f59e0b) for moderate, green (#16a34a) for clear. Severity indicators are the only saturated color. Tabular findings layout with reference-range styling. A monospace face for cited contract text (distinguishing evidence from analysis). A clean authoritative sans for everything else. No gradients, no decorative elements — the data visualization is the design. The signature interaction is a finding row that expands to reveal the full citation and counter-offer, the way a flagged value on a lab report links to its reference range.

**STORY:** The visitor sees a contract being diagnosed. They understand: this reads what they would miss, and shows exactly where it found it. They believe: the analysis is systematic and the evidence is traceable. They act: upload their own contract.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** "Redline" and a single line — "Your contract, diagnosed." — anchored top-left. Below: a rendered diagnostic report of a sample freelancer contract, 3–4 findings visible as rows, each showing the rule name, a severity pill (red or amber), the exact cited sentence from the contract in monospace, and a one-line assessment. One finding expanded to show the counter-offer. The CTA sits below the report: "Try it on your contract." Nothing else in the first viewport.

**FORM:** Medical diagnostic report — position 1 of 7, seed key abd4a8be.

**FINISH:** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
