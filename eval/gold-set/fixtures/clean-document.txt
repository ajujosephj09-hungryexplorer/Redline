FENWICK ROAD COMMUNITY GARDEN: ANNUAL PLOT LICENCE

This Licence is made between the Fenwick Road Garden Association (the “Association”) and the plot holder named at the end of this Licence (the “Plot Holder”) for the 2027 growing season.

1. WHAT IS LICENSED

The Association licenses one numbered plot, measuring approximately ten feet by twenty feet, together with shared use of the tool shed, the compost bays, the standpipes and the mown paths. The plot number is written at the end of this Licence. The Association keeps the freehold and the Plot Holder takes no interest in the land beyond the use described here.

2. TERM

This Licence runs from 1 March 2027 to 30 November 2027. It does not renew. Plots are allocated afresh each January, and a Plot Holder in good standing is welcome to apply again and is considered alongside everyone else who applies.

3. FEE

The fee for the season is forty-ﬁve dollars ($45), payable once, on or before 1 March 2027. The fee is ﬁxed for the whole season and the Association will not raise it during the term. The fee covers water, compost, the shared tools and the Association’s insurance on the tool shed. If the fee has not arrived by 1 March, the plot secretary will contact the Plot Holder before the Association takes any further step.

4. WHAT THE PLOT HOLDER AGREES TO DO

Keep the plot in cultivation, so that at least half of it is planted by 1 June and the rest is either planted or covered. Keep the paths on two sides of the plot clear to a width of eighteen inches. Use no synthetic pesticide or herbicide anywhere on the site. Water from the standpipes with a can or a hose held in the hand, not a sprinkler left running. Take home anything that cannot go in the compost bays. Close and lock the tool shed after use, and put shared tools back clean.

5. WHAT THE ASSOCIATION AGREES TO DO

Keep the standpipes running from 1 March to 30 November, barring a repair or a municipal restriction. Mow the shared paths at least every three weeks through the growing season. Deliver bulk compost to the bays twice in the season and wood chip for the paths once. Keep the tool shed stocked with the tools listed on the inside of its door and replace any that break. Give the Plot Holder at least seven days notice of any work that needs access across the plot.

6. NEIGHBOURS AND DISAGREEMENTS

Plot holders sort out day-to-day matters between themselves where they can. Anything that cannot be settled that way goes to the plot secretary, who will hear both sides at the next monthly meeting and write down what was agreed. Nothing in this Licence limits the right of either the Plot Holder or the Association to take a dispute to any court that would otherwise hear it, or to be represented by anyone they choose.

7. ENDING THIS LICENCE EARLY

The Plot Holder may end this Licence at any time by giving the Association thirty days written notice, and the Association may end it on the same thirty days written notice. Where the Association gives notice, it will refund the fee in proportion to the months left in the season. Where the Plot Holder gives notice, no refund is due, and the Plot Holder may harvest whatever is ready before the notice period runs out.

8. AT THE END OF THE SEASON

By 30 November the Plot Holder clears away canes, netting, frames and anything else brought onto the plot, and leaves perennial plantings in place for the next holder unless the plot secretary agrees they may be lifted. Anything left after 15 December becomes the Association’s to keep or to clear.

9. CHANGES TO THIS LICENCE

This Licence may be changed only in writing, signed by both the Plot Holder and the plot secretary. A change to the site rules that would alter anything written here has no effect on this Licence until it is signed in that way.
