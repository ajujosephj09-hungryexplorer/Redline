MERIDIAN ATHLETIC CLUB — MEMBER SERVICES AND INSTRUCTOR CERTIFICATION AGREEMENT

This Member Services and Instructor Certiﬁcation Agreement (this “Agreement”) is made between Meridian Athletic Club, LLC (“Meridian,” “we” or “us”) and the individual named on the enrolment form (“you” or the “Member”). By signing below, by completing enrolment through the Meridian website, or by using any Meridian facility, you accept each of the terms set out below, including the dispute resolution terms in Section 12.

1. MEMBERSHIP AND ACCESS

Meridian grants you a personal, non-transferable licence to use the facilities at the home location shown on your enrolment form during that location’s posted hours. Posted hours vary by season and on public holidays, and the current hours for each location are shown in the Meridian mobile application. Access is by keycard or through the application, and you are responsible for every entry made with your keycard or your account. You agree to keep the contact details, billing details and emergency contact on your account current, and to tell Member Services in writing of any change within ten (10) days. Guest privileges are issued at Meridian’s discretion, are limited to four (4) visits per guest in any calendar year, and a guest is bound by the Club Rules while on club premises.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

Your initial membership term is twelve (12) months, beginning on the Commencement Date shown on your enrolment form. At the end of the initial term, and at the end of each term thereafter, your membership renews automatically for a further twelve (12) month term at the then-current rate. To stop a renewal you must deliver written notice of cancellation to the Member Services desk no later than three (3) days before the renewal date. Notice delivered after that day takes effect at the end of the term that follows, and the dues for the intervening term remain payable in full. A membership that has been suspended, frozen or placed on medical hold continues to renew on its original schedule.

3. DUES, ADJUSTMENTS AND LATE CHARGES

Monthly dues are the amount stated on your enrolment form and are drawn on the ﬁrst business day of each month from the payment method held on your account. Dues for a partial ﬁrst month are prorated to the Commencement Date and drawn with the second month’s dues. Meridian may adjust dues once in any twelve (12) month period by the greater of six percent (6%)  or the increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve months, on thirty (30) days notice to the email address on your account. Dues not received within ten (10) days of the draw date incur the late charge set out in the Schedule of Fees then in effect, which is posted at the Member Services desk and on the Meridian website and forms part of this Agreement. You authorise Meridian to draw all dues, tuition and charges from the payment method on your account until this Agreement has ended and your balance is nil.

4. CLUB RULES AND CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

You agree to observe the rules posted at each location and in the Meridian mobile application (the “Club Rules”), which cover equipment use, reservation and cancellation of studio sessions, conduct towards staff and other members, and the sessions the timetable marks ‘members only’. Meridian may amend the Club Rules, the Schedule of Fees, the hours of operation, the class timetable and the terms of this Agreement at any time by posting the amended version at the club or on the Meridian website, and the amended version applies to you from the date it is posted. Your continued use of any facility after an amended version is posted is your acceptance of it.

5. FACILITIES, HEALTH AND SAFETY

Meridian services the equipment on the weight-training ﬂoor, in the studios and in the pool hall on a published schedule, and equipment may be withdrawn from use for servicing without notice.   

You conﬁrm that you are in adequate health to undertake the activities you choose, and you accept that strength training, group ﬁtness and swimming carry a risk of injury that no amount of supervision removes. Chalk, outdoor footwear and personal audio without headphones are not permitted in the studios. Personal property is brought onto club premises at your own risk, and lockers are provided for convenience only.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Meridian is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential or punitive damages, for lost income or lost business opportunity, or for loss of or damage to property brought onto club premises, including property left in a locker. Meridian’s total liability to you for any claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, your membership or your use of any facility — whether the claim is framed in contract, in negligence or otherwise — will not exceed the total dues and fees you paid to Meridian in the twelve (12) months immediately before the event giving rise to the claim.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

You will indemnify, defend and hold harmless Meridian and its ofﬁcers, employees, instructors and contractors against any claim, demand, loss, liability or expense, including reasonable legal fees, that is brought by a third party and arises out of your use of the facilities, your conduct on club premises, the conduct of a guest you bring, or any service you provide to another member. Meridian will select counsel, and you will not settle a claim covered by this Section without Meridian’s written consent.

8. INSTRUCTOR CERTIFICATION PROGRAM

Enrolment in the Meridian Instructor Certiﬁcation Program (the “Program”) is optional, is separate from membership, and is open to members in good standing. Tuition is billed annually in advance at the rate shown on your Program enrolment form and covers coursework, two practical assessments and one re-sit. Candidates are expected to complete sixty (60) supervised teaching hours in each Program year, and a candidate who falls short may be held back a year at Meridian’s discretion. Program enrolment renews on each anniversary of your Program start date at the then-current tuition. You may end your Program enrolment by notifying the Program ofﬁce in writing at least ninety (90) days before the anniversary date, in which case no further tuition is charged and your candidacy closes at the end of the Program year then running. Program materials, the curriculum outline, the assessment rubrics and the Meridian coaching manual are conﬁdential and remain Meridian’s property.

9. POST-TERMINATION OBLIGATIONS

For twelve (12) months after your Program candidacy ends for any reason, you will not teach, coach or deliver group ﬁtness instruction or personal training, whether as an employee, as a contractor or on your own account, at any facility within ﬁfteen (15) miles of a Meridian location at which you taught or trained. You will not, during that period, solicit any Meridian member or candidate for instruction outside a Meridian location. You will return the coaching manual and any Meridian teaching materials in your possession within fourteen (14) days of the end of your candidacy.

10. ASSIGNMENT

Meridian may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to a purchaser of the business or of the location at which your membership is held, and your membership continues on the same terms. You may not assign or transfer this Agreement, your membership or your place in the Program to another person.

11. NOTICES

Notice to you is given at the email address or the postal address on your account. Notice to Meridian under Section 2, Section 8 or Section 12 is given at Meridian Athletic Club, LLC, Attention: Member Services, 118 Fenwick Road, Suite 200, Ashlake.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any dispute, claim or controversy between you and Meridian arising out of or relating to this Agreement, your membership or the Program will be resolved by ﬁnal and binding arbitration administered by a nationally recognised arbitration provider under its consumer arbitration rules, and you and Meridian each waive any right to a trial by jury and any right to bring or participate in a class, collective or representative action. You may opt out of this Section 12 by delivering written notice to the address in Section 11 within thirty (30) days after you ﬁrst accept this Agreement, and opting out will not affect your membership or your standing in the Program. Arbitration is conducted by a single arbitrator, the arbitrator decides the scope of this Section, and the award is ﬁnal and may be entered as a judgment.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND SEVERABILITY

This Agreement, the Club Rules and the Schedule of Fees are the entire agreement between you and Meridian and replace anything said or written before you signed. If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in force. Meridian’s failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it.

14. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

You acknowledge that you have read this Agreement, that you have had the opportunity to ask questions about it, and that no representative of Meridian has made any promise to you that is not written in it.
