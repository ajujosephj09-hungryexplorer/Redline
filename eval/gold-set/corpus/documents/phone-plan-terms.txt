CASCADE MOBILE: CONSUMER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between you and Cascade Mobile LLC (“Cascade”). It covers wireless voice, messaging and data service on the rate plan shown on your Service Summary, and any device instalment plan you take out with us. Activating service, or using it, accepts this Agreement.

1. SERVICE AND COVERAGE

Coverage maps show approximate outdoor coverage and are estimates. Service depends on your device, on network capacity, on terrain and on conditions outside our control, and we do not promise uninterrupted service or a particular data speed.

Data speeds on an unlimited plan may be reduced after you use the monthly priority allowance shown on your Service Summary, and reduced speeds continue until the next bill period starts.

2. BILLING

Service is billed monthly in advance. Usage charges, taxes and surcharges are billed after they are incurred. Your first bill may cover a part month and the following full month.

Your bill includes a Regulatory Cost Recovery Charge and an Administrative Charge, neither of which is a tax or a charge required by any government, and Cascade may introduce, increase or remove either charge on notice given with the bill in which the change ﬁrst appears.

An amount not paid by the due date shown on the bill incurs a late payment charge of the greater of five dollars ($5.00) or one and one half percent of the unpaid balance, applied each bill period until the balance is paid, and a restored line incurs the restoral charge shown in the Rate and Charge Guide.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

Service on a monthly plan has no fixed term and continues from bill period to bill period until cancelled.

Service on a twelve month promotional plan renews for a further twelve month term at the end of the term then running, at the standard rate for the plan rather than at the promotional rate, unless you tell us to stop the renewal before the last day of the current term.

Cancelling a promotional plan before the end of its term does not cancel a device instalment plan, and the unpaid instalment balance becomes due in full on your next bill.

4. CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

Cascade may change any term of this Agreement, any charge, and any feature of your rate plan, and the change takes effect on the date stated in the notice we give you, which may be given on a bill, by message to your device, or by email.

Continuing to use the service after the effective date of a change accepts the change. Where a change materially increases your monthly recurring charge, you may cancel the affected line without an early termination charge within thirty days of the notice.

5. OUR LIABILITY

Cascade’s entire liability to you for any claim relating to the service, a device, or this Agreement, whether the claim is made in contract, in negligence, in warranty or under any statute, is limited to the service charges applied to the affected line during the bill period in which the claim arose, and Cascade is not liable for lost profits, lost data, or any indirect or consequential loss.

Nothing in this Section limits liability that cannot be limited as a matter of law.

6. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY FOR CLAIMS

You will indemnify and hold Cascade harmless from any claim, loss or expense, including reasonable legal fees, arising out of your use of the service, out of anything sent from or stored on your device, out of the use of your line by anyone you allow to use it, and out of any charge incurred on your account.

7. HOW WE HANDLE DISPUTES

You and Cascade agree that any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the service or any device will be resolved by individual binding arbitration administered by a nationally recognised arbitration provider under its consumer rules, and both of us give up the right to a jury trial and the right to bring or join a class, collective or representative action.

You may reject this arbitration provision by writing to Cascade Mobile LLC, Dispute Notices, 1200 Willowbank Avenue, within thirty (30) days of activating service, and rejecting it will not affect your service or your rate plan.

8. ENDING SERVICE

You may cancel a line at any time by calling us or through the account portal. Charges accrue to the end of the bill period in which you cancel, and a part bill period is not prorated.

We may suspend or end service where an amount is unpaid, where the service is used in a way this Agreement prohibits, or where we are required to by law.

9. NOTICES AND RECORDS

Notices to you may be sent to the billing address, the email address or the mobile number on the account. Calls with our support team may be recorded for training and quality.
