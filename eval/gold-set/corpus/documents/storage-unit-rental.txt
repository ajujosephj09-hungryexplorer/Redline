HARBOUR ROAD SELF STORAGE: UNIT RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Unit Rental Agreement is made between Harbour Road Self Storage LLC (“Operator”) and the person named on the Unit Schedule (“Occupant”). The Unit Schedule records the unit number, the unit size, the monthly rent and the move-in date, and it forms part of this Agreement.

1. USE OF THE UNIT

The Operator rents the Occupant space only. This is not a lease of real property and it is not a warehouse receipt. The Operator does not take custody of anything the Occupant stores and is not a warehouseman.

The Occupant may store lawful personal property only. The Occupant may not store food, live animals, fuel, ammunition, tyres, anything that gives off an odour, or anything flammable, corrosive or explosive. The Occupant may not live in the unit, run a business from it, or work on a vehicle inside it.

2. TERM

The initial term runs from the move-in date to the last day of that calendar month, and rent for a part month is prorated.

After the initial term this Agreement continues on a month to month basis, each month renewing on the first day of the month without notice from either party, until it is ended as Section 8 describes.

3. RENT, ADJUSTMENTS AND CHARGES

Rent is due in advance on the first day of each month, without demand and without deduction. The Operator may apply any payment received to the oldest amount outstanding.

The Operator may increase the monthly rent on not less than thirty (30) days written notice to the Occupant, and the increased rent is due on the first rent day falling after the notice period ends.

Rent not received by the sixth day of the month incurs the late charge shown in the Schedule of Charges posted in the ofﬁce and on the Harbour Road website, and each further notice the Operator is required to send about an unpaid balance incurs the administration charge shown in the same schedule.

4. INSURANCE AND VALUE

The Occupant is required to keep insurance on the stored property for its full value and to produce evidence of it on request. Where the Occupant does not, the Occupant carries the risk of loss and the Operator is not an insurer of anything in the unit.

The Operator's total liability to the Occupant for any loss of or damage to stored property, however caused, including loss or damage caused by the Operator's own negligence, is limited to five thousand dollars ($5,000) unless the Occupant has declared a higher value on the Unit Schedule and paid the corresponding value protection fee.

5. THIRD PARTY CLAIMS

The Occupant will indemnify, defend and hold the Operator harmless from every claim, demand, loss, cost and expense, including reasonable attorney fees, brought by any person and arising out of the Occupant's use of the unit or the premises, the property stored in the unit, or the acts of anyone the Occupant allows on to the premises.

6. ACCESS, RULES AND CHANGES

Gate hours, the rules posted in the office, and the terms of this Agreement may be changed by the Operator on notice posted at the facility and sent to the Occupant's last known email address, and the changed version applies to the Occupant from the first day of the month after it is posted.

The Operator may enter the unit to inspect it, to repair the premises, or where the Operator reasonably believes the contents present a danger, and will leave notice of the entry in or on the unit.

7. DEFAULT AND LIEN

Where rent or any charge is unpaid, the Operator has a lien on all property stored in the unit and may deny the Occupant access, overlock the unit, and after the notice period the law requires, sell the contents and apply the proceeds to what is owed.

8. ENDING THIS AGREEMENT

The Occupant may end this Agreement by giving written notice at least ten days before the end of a month, removing all property and leaving the unit swept clean. The Operator may end this Agreement on thirty days written notice.

Rent already paid for the month in which the Occupant moves out is not refunded.

9. NOTICES

Notices to the Occupant go to the postal address and email address on the Unit Schedule. It is the Occupant's responsibility to keep both current.
