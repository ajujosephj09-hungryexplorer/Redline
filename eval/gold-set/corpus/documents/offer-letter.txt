ARDENT BIOSCIENCES INC: OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Dear Candidate,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Senior Process Chemist at Ardent Biosciences Inc, reporting to the Director of Process Development at our Fern Hollow site. This letter sets out the terms of the offer. Please sign and return it by 3 April 2026.

1. POSITION, START DATE AND COMPENSATION

Your start date is 4 May 2026, subject to a satisfactory background check and to your producing documents establishing your right to work. Your annual base salary is $148,000, paid semi-monthly, less applicable withholding.

You are eligible for the annual discretionary bonus scheme at a target of twelve percent of base salary. Bonus awards are discretionary in amount and in whether they are made at all, and an award is paid only if you are employed on the payment date.

You will receive a one-time signing payment of $15,000 with your first pay cycle. If you resign or are terminated for cause within twelve (12) months of your start date, the full signing payment becomes repayable within thirty days of your last working day.

2. EMPLOYMENT AT WILL

Your employment is at will. Either you or Ardent may end it at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Nothing in this letter is a contract of employment for a fixed term.

3. POLICIES, HANDBOOK AND PLAN DOCUMENTS

Your employment is governed by the Ardent Employee Handbook, the Code of Conduct, and the plan documents for each benefit you enrol in.

Ardent may introduce, withdraw, interpret or amend any policy, handbook provision, benefit plan or term of employment described in this letter at any time and without your agreement, and the version in force on the Ardent intranet governs from the date it is published there.

4. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND INVENTIONS

On or before your start date you will sign the Employee Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement enclosed with this letter. It survives the end of your employment.

You confirm that you are not subject to any obligation to a former employer that would prevent you from performing this role, and that you will not bring to Ardent or use in your work any confidential information belonging to anyone else.

5. POST-TERMINATION OBLIGATIONS

For a period of twelve (12) months following the last day of your employment, whether that employment ends at your choice or at ours, you will not provide services that are the same as or substantially similar to those you provided to Ardent, whether as an employee, a consultant, a contractor, an adviser or a principal, to any business that develops, manufactures or sells a product within the Restricted Field as that term is defined in Exhibit B to this letter.

For the same period you will not solicit, or assist anyone else in soliciting, any employee of Ardent with whom you worked in your final twelve months, and you will not approach any customer, distributor or contract manufacturer of Ardent that you dealt with in that period for the purpose of providing a product or service in the Restricted Field.

The geographic scope of both obligations is set out in Exhibit B. You acknowledge that these obligations are reasonable, that they are supported by the signing payment described in Section 1, and that a breach of either would cause Ardent harm that money alone would not repair.

6. INDEMNITY AND THIRD PARTY CLAIMS

You will indemnify Ardent and hold it harmless against any claim, loss, liability or expense, including reasonable attorney fees, that arises out of a breach by you of Section 4 or Section 5 of this letter, or out of a claim by a former employer that your employment with Ardent breaches an obligation you owe to them.

7. DISPUTES

You and Ardent each agree that any claim arising out of or relating to your employment, its terms, or its end, including any statutory claim, will be resolved by final and binding arbitration administered under the employment rules of a nationally recognised arbitration provider, on an individual basis only, and each of us waives any right to a jury trial and any right to participate in a class, collective or representative action.

This Section does not prevent either of us from seeking a temporary restraining order or preliminary injunction from a court in aid of arbitration.

8. ACCEPTANCE

This letter, the Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement and Exhibit B are the entire agreement about your employment and replace any earlier discussion, written or spoken.

Sincerely,

Head of Talent, Ardent Biosciences Inc
