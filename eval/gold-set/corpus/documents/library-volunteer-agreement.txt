HATHERLEIGH PUBLIC LIBRARY: VOLUNTEER AGREEMENT

This Agreement records what the Hatherleigh Public Library and the volunteer named below have agreed about volunteer work during the 2026 season, which runs from 1 April to 31 October 2026.

1. WHAT THE VOLUNTEER WILL DO

The volunteer will work one shift of three hours each week, on the day agreed with the Volunteer Coordinator at the start of the season. Shifts are shelving, the returns desk, the children's reading hour, or the Saturday book sale, as the roster shows.

The volunteer will sign in at the staff desk at the start of a shift and sign out at the end of it. Where the volunteer cannot work a shift, the volunteer will tell the Coordinator at least twenty-four hours ahead so the roster can be changed.

The volunteer will treat borrower records as confidential and will not discuss what any named person has borrowed.

2. WHAT THE LIBRARY WILL DO

The Library will give the volunteer an induction before the first shift, covering the catalogue system, the fire exits and who to ask for help.

The Library will name a staff member as the contact for each shift and will publish the roster two weeks ahead.

The Library will reimburse bus fare for a shift on production of the ticket, up to eight dollars a shift, paid in the month after the claim.

The Library will provide tea, coffee and the use of the staff room during a shift.

3. TRAINING

The volunteer may attend any staff training session that has a free place, at no cost. The Library will tell volunteers about a session at the same time it tells staff.

4. THIS IS NOT EMPLOYMENT

Volunteering is unpaid and is not employment. Neither the Library nor the volunteer intends this Agreement to create an employment relationship, and the volunteer is not entitled to a wage, to holiday pay or to a pension contribution.

5. ENDING THE ARRANGEMENT

Either the volunteer or the Library may end this Agreement at any time by telling the other, in person or in writing. Nothing needs to be paid by either side when it ends.

This Agreement ends on 31 October 2026 whatever else happens. Volunteering in the 2027 season means signing a new agreement for that season.

6. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

A volunteer who is unhappy about something may raise it with the Volunteer Coordinator, and if it is not resolved there, with the Head Librarian, who will answer in writing within ten working days.

The Library's own insurance covers volunteers working a rostered shift on library premises. The volunteer does not need to arrange cover.

7. REFERENCES

On request the Library will write a reference describing the shifts the volunteer worked and the length of service.
