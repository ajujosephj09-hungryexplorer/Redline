LOWELL AND KERR LLP: SUMMER ANALYST PROGRAMME AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Lowell and Kerr LLP (“the Firm”) and the person named on the Programme Schedule (“the Analyst”), and covers the twelve week Summer Analyst Programme beginning on the date shown on that Schedule.

1. THE PROGRAMME

The Analyst will be assigned to a desk in the Research group and will work under the supervision of a named associate. The Programme includes a weekly teaching session, two written assessments and an end of programme review.

The Programme is a fixed term engagement and carries no offer or promise of further employment. The Firm may end the Programme early for any reason on one week written notice.

2. STIPEND AND HOUSING ADVANCE

The Analyst receives the weekly stipend shown on the Programme Schedule, paid on the Friday of each week worked, less applicable withholding.

Where the Analyst takes the optional housing advance, it is repaid in six equal instalments deducted from the stipend, and any instalment not recovered within ten (10) days of its due date carries the late charge set out in the Programme Handbook until it is repaid in full.

3. HANDBOOK AND CHANGES

The Analyst will observe the Programme Handbook, the Firm's Information Security Policy and the Research group's publication rules.

The Firm may amend the Programme Handbook, the assessment structure, the teaching schedule and the terms of this Agreement at any time by issuing a revised version to the Analyst by email, and the revised version applies from the date of that email.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Analyst will keep confidential every client name, model, memorandum and data set the Analyst sees during the Programme, and will not retain a copy of any of it after the Programme ends. This obligation has no end date.

5. POST-PROGRAMME OBLIGATIONS

For six (6) months after the Programme ends, the Analyst will not accept an engagement, whether paid or unpaid and whether as an employee, a contractor or an intern, with any firm on the Restricted List issued to the Analyst in week one of the Programme, and will not approach any client of the Firm whose file the Analyst worked on.

The Analyst acknowledges that the Restricted List is confidential and that it may be reissued during the Programme.

6. THIRD PARTY CLAIMS

The Analyst will indemnify the Firm and hold it harmless against any claim, loss or expense, including reasonable legal costs, arising out of a breach by the Analyst of Section 4 or Section 5, or out of anything the Analyst publishes about the Firm or a client of the Firm.

7. DISPUTES

Any dispute between the Analyst and the Firm arising out of the Programme or out of this Agreement will be resolved by final and binding arbitration on an individual basis, administered under the employment rules of a nationally recognised provider, and the Analyst gives up any right to a jury trial and any right to bring or join a class or collective action.

8. PROPERTY AND RETURN

The laptop, the security token and the building pass issued to the Analyst remain the property of the Firm and are returned on the last day of the Programme. An item not returned within five working days is charged at the replacement cost shown in the Programme Handbook.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, the Programme Schedule and the Programme Handbook are the whole of what is agreed about the Programme.
