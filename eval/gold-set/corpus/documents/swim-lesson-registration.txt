BRIARFIELD AQUATIC CENTRE: LEARN TO SWIM REGISTRATION

This registration covers one place in the Learn to Swim programme for the eight week session named on the registration card. It sets out what the Centre provides, what the fee is, and how a place can be changed or given up.

1. THE SESSION

A session is eight lessons of thirty minutes, held on the same day and at the same time each week. Lessons run in the shallow pool with a maximum of six children to one instructor.

The session named on the registration card ends after the eighth lesson. There is no continuation and no further charge, and a place in the next session is arranged by registering again when bookings open.

2. THE FEE

The fee is one hundred and twenty dollars for the session, payable in full when the place is booked. The fee is the same for every session in 2026 and covers the eight lessons, pool entry on lesson days, and the end of session progress card.

Nothing else is charged. There is no joining fee, no equipment charge and no charge for a make-up lesson arranged under Section 4.

3. IF THE CENTRE CANCELS A LESSON

Where the Centre cancels a lesson, for a pool closure, an instructor absence or a plant fault, the Centre will offer a make-up lesson in the same session or the one following. Where no make-up lesson can be offered, the Centre refunds fifteen dollars for the cancelled lesson.

The Centre will tell the parent or guardian by text message as soon as a cancellation is known, and will post it at the reception desk.

4. IF A CHILD MISSES A LESSON

A lesson missed for illness may be made up in another class at the same level during the same session, where a place is free. Ask at reception, and bring a note of the missed date.

Two or more missed lessons in a row may be carried into the following session at no charge, on the Centre's agreement.

5. GIVING UP A PLACE

A place given up more than seven days before the first lesson is refunded in full. A place given up later than that, and before the third lesson, is refunded at half the fee. After the third lesson no refund is made.

A place may be transferred once to another child in the same household at no charge, by telling reception before the fourth lesson.

6. WHAT TO BRING, AND THE POOL RULES

Bring a swimsuit, a towel and a pair of goggles. Children who are not reliably toilet trained wear a swim nappy. Long hair is tied back or covered.

A parent or guardian stays in the building for the whole lesson and watches from the viewing gallery rather than the poolside, so that the instructor has the pool edge clear.

7. HOW TO REACH US

Reception answers the telephone between 07:00 and 21:00 every day, and email within one working day. The programme coordinator is available in person on Tuesday and Thursday mornings.
