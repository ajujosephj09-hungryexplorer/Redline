# Existing Contract Review & Clause Analysis Products

Research conducted: 2026-08-28

---

**Product:** Spellbook (by Rally)
**What it does:** Microsoft Word add-in that drafts, reviews, and redlines contracts inline. Offers AI-generated clause suggestions, term comparison, and a sidebar assistant that flags risks and recommends edits without leaving Word.
**Target:** Solo lawyers and small-to-mid-size transactional law firms that live in Microsoft Word.
**Price:** Approximately $99/user/month at entry level; higher tiers available; no free tier — 7-day trial requires a sales conversation.
**Main complaint:** Locked entirely to the Microsoft Word workflow; limited for analyzing counterparty documents or running high-volume portfolio reviews; drafting-focused rather than review-focused.
**Source:** https://thelegalprompts.com/blog/best-ai-contract-review-tools-2026

---

**Product:** LegalOn
**What it does:** Standalone web app offering playbook-based automated contract review. Attorney-written playbooks score contracts against a company's standard positions, flag deviations, generate redline suggestions, and provide risk ratings clause-by-clause.
**Target:** In-house legal teams at startups through mid-market companies handling recurring contract types (NDAs, MSAs, vendor agreements).
**Price:** $3,000–$8,000/year for smaller teams; individual plan ~$550/month billed annually; custom pricing at enterprise scale.
**Main complaint:** Requires significant upfront playbook configuration before the AI is useful; not cost-effective for teams reviewing fewer than 5 contracts per month; less flexible for non-standard or novel contract types.
**Source:** https://www.legalontech.com/post/best-ai-contract-review-tools

---

**Product:** Kira Systems (now part of Litera)
**What it does:** Enterprise provision-extraction engine that identifies and pulls 1,000+ clause and provision types across large contract populations. Primarily used for M&A due diligence and bulk contract analysis — surfaces anomalies rather than redlining individual documents.
**Target:** Large law firms and corporate legal departments running M&A due diligence or portfolio-wide contract audits.
**Price:** Enterprise-only; reported $50,000–$100,000 annually.
**Main complaint:** Extraction-focused, not redline-focused — does not suggest edits or counter-offers; requires high deal volume to justify cost; overkill for routine commercial contracts.
**Source:** https://thelegalprompts.com/blog/best-ai-contract-review-tools-2026

---

**Product:** Harvey
**What it does:** Broad enterprise generalist legal AI that handles contract analysis alongside litigation support, regulatory research, and corporate law tasks. Acts as a conversational AI assistant across multiple practice areas rather than a dedicated contract tool.
**Target:** Am Law 100 firms and top-tier enterprise legal departments needing multi-task AI across practice areas.
**Price:** ~$1,000–$2,000/seat/month; annual contracts commonly $50,000–$300,000+; demo-only access (no self-serve).
**Main complaint:** Not purpose-built for contract review; general-purpose architecture may lack the specialized depth of dedicated clause-analysis tools; 2–6 month implementation time; extremely expensive.
**Source:** https://www.legalontech.com/post/best-ai-contract-review-tools

---

**Product:** CoCounsel (Thomson Reuters)
**What it does:** Conversational AI legal assistant offering contract review alongside legal research and drafting. Integrates with Westlaw for citation-grounded answers; can summarize contracts, extract key terms, and flag risks via chat interface.
**Target:** Law firms and legal teams already subscribed to Thomson Reuters / Westlaw products.
**Price:** Four tiers reported at approximately $104–$639/user/month; frequently bundled with Westlaw subscriptions.
**Main complaint:** Real all-in cost runs meaningfully higher than the headline seat price once bundling and add-ons are factored in; contract review is one feature among many rather than a dedicated workflow; value degrades if not using Westlaw.
**Source:** https://thelegalprompts.com/blog/best-ai-contract-review-tools-2026

---

**Product:** Luminance
**What it does:** AI platform for automated contract markup and redlining at scale. Uses pattern recognition to identify anomalies across hundreds or thousands of contracts simultaneously; deploys automated redline agents for high-volume M&A data-room work.
**Target:** Large law firms and enterprise M&A teams needing rapid data-room population and portfolio review.
**Price:** Quote-based; demo-only access; no public list price; reported $100,000+ for enterprise deployments.
**Main complaint:** Enterprise-only engagement model with no transparent pricing makes budget planning impossible for smaller practices; overly complex and cost-prohibitive for routine commercial contract review (NDAs, vendor agreements).
**Source:** https://thelegalprompts.com/blog/best-ai-contract-review-tools-2026

---

**Product:** LawGeex
**What it does:** Contract review automation platform that reviews incoming business contracts against pre-configured company legal policies. Flags deviations from approved positions, suggests approved alternative language, and handles high-volume, low-value contract processing (e.g., procurement NDAs) to reduce lawyer time on first-pass review.
**Target:** F1000 companies and large enterprises (e.g., eBay, HP) with high volumes of standardized, recurring contracts; legal operations teams seeking to reduce attorney time on routine review.
**Price:** Custom enterprise pricing only; no public list price. Positioned at the enterprise tier ($30,000+ annually estimated).
**Main complaint:** Requires extensive pre-configuration of legal policies before the AI adds value; mindshare remains low (2.7% in AI for Legal Affairs category as of 2026) suggesting limited adoption outside large enterprises; not suited to small teams or varied contract types.
**Source:** https://www.getapp.com/operations-management-software/a/lawgeex/

---

**Product:** The Legal Prompts (Contract Risk Analyzer)
**What it does:** Web-based clause-by-clause risk scoring tool. Analyzes contracts from the perspective of the user's role (buyer, seller, licensor, etc.), assigns risk scores to individual clauses, and provides recommended fixes. Designed for affordable, defensible first-pass review without enterprise infrastructure.
**Target:** Solo attorneys and small firms seeking affordable contract review; individuals who cannot justify enterprise pricing.
**Price:** Starter $29/month, Professional $49/month, Strategic $99/month. Free tier not confirmed.
**Main complaint:** Limited to single-document review; not designed for high-volume portfolio analysis; lacks the integration depth (CLM, Word add-in, API) of enterprise tools; smaller brand with limited third-party reviews.
**Source:** https://thelegalprompts.com/blog/best-ai-contract-review-tools-2026
